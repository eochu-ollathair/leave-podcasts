A short morning podcast report

Leave the Podcasts.
Get the point of each show.

Choose the podcasts you want, how many recent episodes to read, and how many points to get from each podcast. The starting choice is three points per podcast. Each point says why it matters. Your sceptical reading sits beneath that podcast, where it can name the exact claim it concerns.

How it works

  • Finds recent episodes from the podcasts you choose.
  • Reads their written speech when available. Otherwise, your computer listens to the episode and turns it into words.
  • With your own optional artificial intelligence program, it covers different subjects in each episode and puts your sceptical reading beneath each podcast.
  • Clears episodes after sending them and never puts the same episode in another report.

The free version quotes actual speech and needs no artificial intelligence account. It cannot reliably explain a hidden motive. To check a speaker's claim, follow its named study or ask for the records behind it.

Get your own copy

This is a computer program, not a phone app. It currently starts on Mac or Linux. It needs Python on that computer and your own Telegram bot. Keep the computer on for morning reports.

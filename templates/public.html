A short morning podcast report

Leave the Podcasts.
Get the information in three lines.

Choose the podcasts you want and how many recent episodes to read. Each morning, get three short Telegram lines about what was said. With your own AI, your sceptical take on why it matters goes at the end. You can preview the report and send one yourself.

How it works

  • Finds recent episodes from the podcasts you choose.
  • Reads their written speech when available. Otherwise, your computer listens to the episode and turns it into words.
  • Uses your own optional AI to explain why each claim matters, then puts your sceptical angle at the end.

The free version quotes actual speech and needs no AI account. It cannot reliably explain a hidden motive. Claims made by speakers are not independently proven.

Get your own copy

This is a computer program, not a phone app. It currently starts on Mac or Linux. It needs Python on that computer and your own Telegram bot. Keep the computer on for morning reports.
